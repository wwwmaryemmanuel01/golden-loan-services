2.3

How the cut works

  • The cut is a photograph at 06:00 on the cut day — movements after the cut belong to next month's statement.
  • A loan statement is cut per loan; a savings statement per account; a client may hold several.
  • The calendar is locked a month ahead — a cut only moves by committee minute, recorded in the audit trail.
  • Certificates are cut quarterly; the company pack follows the close by five working days.

Interest posting, in the open

  • Savings interest is calculated daily on the closing balance and posted monthly — the statement shows both steps.

How a build runs

  • The build photographs the ledger at the cut — movements after the cut wait for next month.
  • Every statement is numbered before it is rendered, so a failed build can never duplicate a reference.
  • A build that fails rolls back whole — there is never a half-built month on the shelf.
  • The batch log keeps the count, the timing and the checksum of every build.

Wording rules

  • The client is addressed by name, never by account number, in the salutation.

The correction rules

  • A statement is never edited — it is reissued with a new reference, and the old one is marked superseded.
  • The correction note travels on the reissue: the client sees what changed and why.
  • Both versions stay in the archive forever — the audit trail has no delete button.
  • A correction caused by our error is delivered with an apology, in writing, on the letter.

15.2

How reconciliation works

The ruleEvery line on every statement must sit on the ledger — one to one, by reference.
The gapsA gap means a statement was cut before a late posting landed, or a line was corrected.
The fixA gap never edits the statement; it schedules a reissue under CR-….
The proofThe reconciliation run is itself logged, with its counts and its checksum.
15.3

When the gap is ours

  • Three savings statements are open this month — late interest postings landed after the cut.
  • The clients are told first; the reissue follows the same day the reconciliation closes.
  • The tellers' cut-off is being moved five minutes earlier so the November cut cannot repeat this.

17.2

How the archive works

  • Every statement is immutable in the archive — a reissue is a new document, never an overwrite.
  • Retrieval is by reference, client, period or batch — under a minute, tested quarterly.
  • The archive is replicated; a lost data centre cannot lose a statement.
  • At year seven a statement is reviewed, not deleted — legal holds keep anything in dispute forever.
17.3

The legal note

Statements are evidence. The archive exists so that any account, any month, any line can be produced to the client, the auditor, the regulator or the court — complete, unaltered, and with its delivery history attached.
