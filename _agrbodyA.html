1 Parties and definitions

“We”, “us”, “the lender” means Golden Financial Services, its successors and assigns. “You”, “the borrower” means the person named on the application form and on the offer letter. “Guarantor” means any person who signs a deed of guarantee for your loan. “Security” means any asset pledged, charged or assigned to us. “Instalment” means the amount due on a repayment date, made up of principal, interest, fees and any charges then payable.

2 The offer, and when this agreement begins

Your application is not an offer to lend and does not create any obligation on us to lend. If your application is approved we will issue a written offer letter stating the amount, the term, the interest, the fees, the repayment dates and any security required. This agreement binds both of us only when you have accepted these terms, signed the offer letter, and we have advanced the funds. We may decline any application without giving reasons, and we will tell you what could strengthen a future application.

No fee is ever payable before an offer is issued and accepted. If anyone asks you for money to “process” or “release” a loan, do not pay it and report it to us at once.

3 Amount, term, interest and fees

Interest is charged at 6.5% per month on the declining balance — that is, on the amount of principal still outstanding, not on the original amount. Interest accrues daily and is charged on each repayment date. The arrangement fee of 3% of the amount advanced is charged once and is disclosed on the offer letter before you accept. Any statutory levy or tax that applies will be shown separately and is not ours to keep.

  • The repayment amount and dates are set out in the repayment schedule attached to the offer letter.
  • Where the term is extended at your request and we agree, a rescheduling fee of MK 50,000 applies.
  • Statements are available at any branch, and on request by SMS or email, at no charge.

4 Repayment

You must pay each instalment on or before its due date, by the method shown on the schedule. A payment is only treated as made when the funds have cleared to us. Payments are applied first to any charges, then to interest, then to principal, unless we tell you otherwise in writing.

If a payment is late a late-payment charge of 2% per month on the overdue instalment applies from the day after the due date, and the arrears are reported to the credit reference bureau. Repeated late payment is a default under clause 8.

5 Guarantors and security

You must provide at least one guarantor acceptable to us, and any security we require. A guarantor signs a deed of guarantee and is liable for the whole outstanding balance if you do not pay; we may pursue the guarantor without first pursuing you. Security is valued by a valuer we appoint, at your cost where the offer letter says so, and we may require it to be insured with our interest noted.

  • You must not sell, pledge, lease or otherwise deal with any security without our written consent.
  • You must keep insured security insured, and pay the premiums, for as long as the loan runs.
  • Where title documents are held, we hold them to your order and return them when the loan is settled and all charges are paid.
  • If security falls in value, or becomes uninsured, we may ask for further security or reduce the term.

6 What you must do

  • Give true, complete and current information, and tell us within 14 days if it changes — a new address, a new job, a new loan elsewhere, or a change in your business.
  • Use the money for the purpose stated in the application. Using it for anything else is a breach of this agreement.
  • Tell us immediately if you expect to miss a payment, and co-operate with any reasonable restructuring proposal.
  • Allow our officers to inspect any security on reasonable notice, and to contact your employer or bank where you have given consent.
  • Do not give false documents. Presenting a forged payslip, title deed or identity document is an offence and we will report it to the police.

7 What we must do

  • Give you a copy of this agreement, the offer letter and the repayment schedule before you sign.
  • Give you a receipt for every payment, numbered and matched to the allocation behind it.
  • Not change the interest rate or the fees during the term, except where the law requires it or where you agree to a restructure in writing.
  • Keep your information confidential and use it only as this agreement and the Data Protection Act allow.
  • Respond to a complaint within 14 days, and tell you how to escalate it if you are not satisfied.

8 Default, recall and enforcement

You are in default if you fail to pay an instalment and remain in arrears for 30 days, if you give false information, if you dispose of security without consent, if you become insolvent, or if you die and no estate or guarantor can meet the debt. On default we may:

  • declare the whole outstanding balance immediately due, with accrued interest and charges;
  • call on your guarantor or guarantors for the whole balance;
  • enforce our charge over the security, including taking possession and selling it, applying the proceeds to the debt and recovering any shortfall from you;
  • report the default to the credit reference bureau, which may affect your ability to borrow elsewhere;
  • recover reasonable legal and tracing costs from you, where the law allows.
Think before you sign. Enforcing security can mean losing a vehicle, land or a house. If you are struggling, contact us before the account goes into default — restructuring is almost always cheaper than enforcement for both of us.

9 Paying early, and settling

You may pay the loan off early at any time, in whole or in part, without a penalty. Where you settle early, interest stops accruing on the day the final cleared payment is received, and we will give you a settlement statement and a release of security within 10 working days.

10 Your information, and credit bureaus

You consent to us collecting, holding and processing your personal and financial information, and the information of your guarantors, to assess this application, administer the loan, meet our legal duties and recover any debt. You consent to us searching a licensed credit reference bureau before and during the loan, and to reporting the loan, its performance and any default to that bureau. You may ask for a copy of the information we hold about you, and ask us to correct anything wrong.

  • We do not sell your information. We share it only with our staff who need it, our valuers and insurers, the credit bureau, and a court or regulator where the law requires.
  • We keep records for seven years after the loan closes, or longer where the law requires.
  • You may withdraw consent to marketing at any time; this does not stop us administering a loan you have taken.

11 Complaints and disputes

Raise a complaint at any branch, by phone or by email, and we will acknowledge it within 3 working days and answer within 14. If you are not satisfied you may escalate to our Managing Director, and then to the Reserve Bank of Malawi's consumer protection unit. This agreement is governed by the laws of Malawi and the courts of Malawi have jurisdiction.

12 Changes, and the whole agreement

We may change these terms where the law changes, or with your written agreement. A change will not apply to a loan already advanced unless the law requires it. This agreement, the offer letter and the repayment schedule together are the whole agreement between us, and replace anything said or written before. If any clause is found to be unenforceable the rest continue to apply.

13 Declarations before you accept

By accepting below you confirm that: you are 18 or older; you have read and understood this agreement, including the clauses on interest, default and security; you have had the chance to take advice; the information in your application is true and complete; and you accept that the loan is repayable whether or not the purpose for which you borrowed succeeds.
