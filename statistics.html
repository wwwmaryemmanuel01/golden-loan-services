How collection is measured

A repayment is collected when the money is confirmed by the channel and matched to the loan. An instalment is on time when it lands on or before its due date. Prepayments are counted in the month they arrive; arrears recoveries are counted against the month the instalment fell due, so the rate never flatters the collector.

How income is recognised

  • Interest is recognised as it is earned — day by day on the outstanding balance.
  • A fee is recognised when it is earned: the application fee when the loan is approved, the insurance fee when the cover starts.
  • Penalties are recognised when they are levied and reversed in full when waived.
  • Write-offs reverse the unpaid interest the day the loan is written off.

What each check proves
