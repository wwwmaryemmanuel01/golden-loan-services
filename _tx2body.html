2.3

The states a transaction moves through

  • Pending — captured or requested, waiting on the provider or a second authorisation.
  • Successful — confirmed by the provider, the receipt is issued and the ledger is updated.
  • Unreconciled — successful, but the provider statement has not confirmed it yet.
  • Failed — the provider refused it; the reason is recorded and a retry may be scheduled.
  • Cancelled — abandoned before submission; no money moved.
  • Reversed — succeeded, then pulled back by a reversal or a provider correction.

Timeout and retry policy

  • A wallet request that is not confirmed in 90 seconds is polled, never re-sent blind.
  • If the poll cannot see the transaction after 3 attempts it is marked unreconciled for a person.
  • A timeout never triggers a second payout — the idempotency key forbids it.
  • Customers are told the true state: pending means pending, not failed.

15.3

The reversal workflow

  • A reversal is a contra row that cites the original — nothing is ever deleted.
  • Provider-initiated reversals are matched to the original by reference and posted automatically.
  • Staff-initiated reversals need a supervisor authorisation and a written reason.
  • The customer's receipt is marked void; the confirmation records the reversal.
